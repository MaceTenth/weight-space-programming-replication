**Strategy.** The paddle moves one cell per step, the same speed as the ball's sideways movement. That means one rule is enough: put the paddle where the ball will be on the next step, so it stays one column ahead of the ball.

- **Why this works:** the ball is caught if, after the paddle moves, the paddle is under the ball's current column or its next column.
- **Error margin:** staying one column ahead means a missed move (a sticky repeat or a softmax sample) still leaves the paddle under the ball. The lead comes back whenever the ball changes sideways direction.
- **Target column:** in general the target is T = 2·ball_x − trail_x, which is the ball's column plus its direction.
- **Wall case:** when the ball is stuck against a wall for one step, the trail is in the same column as the ball. The ball is then about to move away from the wall, so the target becomes the column next to the wall. This is done by changing the weights on ball columns 0 and 9.
- **Paddle-bounce case:** when the ball and trail are both in row 8, the ball has just bounced off the paddle. If it hit the paddle's "next column" side, its sideways direction flipped, and the trail no longer shows which way it is moving. In this case the network uses s′ = ball_x + trail_x − 2·paddle_x. This gives the correct direction whether the paddle is under the ball (direction flipped) or under the trail (direction unchanged).

**Hidden units.**
- **Layer 1:**
  - **Unit 0 (move right):** on when s = T − paddle_x ≥ 1.
  - **Unit 1 (move left):** on when s ≤ −1.
  - **Units 2 and 3:** the same right/left tests for the bounce formula s′.
  - **Unit 4 (gate G):** on when both the ball and the trail are in row 8.
- **Layer 2:**
  - **Unit 0:** right AND NOT G.
  - **Unit 1:** left AND NOT G.
  - **Unit 2:** right′ AND G.
  - **Unit 3:** left′ AND G.
- **Outputs:**
  - **Right logit:** 8·(unit 0 + unit 2), which is about 0 when moving right is wanted and about −16 otherwise. The left logit works the same way.
  - **No-op:** fixed at −8.
  - **Up, down and fire:** fixed at −30.
